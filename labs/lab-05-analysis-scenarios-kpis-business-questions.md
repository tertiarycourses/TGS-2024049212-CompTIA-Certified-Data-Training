# Lab 05 - Analysis Scenarios, KPIs, and Business Questions

## Objectives

- Translate business questions into metrics.
- Define KPIs.
- Compare segments.
- Write answer-first findings.

## Steps

1. Write three business questions your dataset can answer.
2. Define one metric for each question.
3. Write numerator, denominator, filters, and time period.
4. Calculate the KPI values.
5. Compare KPIs across two or more segments.
6. Identify best and worst performers.
7. Check sample size for each segment.
8. Create a findings table with metric, result, interpretation, and caveat.
9. Write one evidence-based recommendation.
10. Write one follow-up question.

## Validation

- KPIs have clear definitions.
- Calculations match the definitions.
- Segment comparisons include sample size.
- Recommendation follows from evidence.

## Review Questions

1. Why define numerator and denominator?
2. Why does sample size matter?
3. What makes a finding answer-first?
4. What is a useful caveat?
