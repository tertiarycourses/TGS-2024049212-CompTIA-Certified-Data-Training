# Lab 07 - Data Governance, Quality, Controls, and Privacy

## Objectives

- Define governance roles.
- Create data quality rules.
- Document lineage and access.
- Identify privacy risks.

## Steps

1. Identify dataset owner and steward.
2. Draw data lineage from source to dashboard.
3. Define quality rules for completeness, validity, consistency, uniqueness, and timeliness.
4. Identify sensitive or personal fields.
5. Decide what should be masked, removed, or restricted.
6. Define access roles: viewer, editor, analyst, and administrator.
7. Create an access control matrix.
8. Define retention and deletion expectations.
9. Identify approval or audit points.
10. Add governance caveats to your dashboard notes.

## Validation

- Lineage shows source and transformation path.
- Quality rules are measurable.
- Sensitive fields are identified.
- Access matrix follows least privilege.

## Review Questions

1. What is data lineage?
2. Why does data ownership matter?
3. What is least privilege?
4. How does retention policy reduce risk?
