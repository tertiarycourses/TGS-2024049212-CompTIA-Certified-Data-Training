# Lab 08 - Capstone Analysis and Performance-Based Exam Review

## Objectives

- Complete an end-to-end analysis.
- Produce findings and visuals.
- Add governance notes.
- Build an exam study plan.

## Steps

1. Start with a raw or trainer-provided dataset.
2. Create a data dictionary.
3. Profile the data.
4. Clean the dataset.
5. Write at least three SQL queries or spreadsheet summaries.
6. Calculate at least three KPIs.
7. Create at least three charts.
8. Build a one-page dashboard or report.
9. Add two data quality caveats.
10. Add one governance recommendation.
11. Present findings in five minutes.
12. Map weak areas to Data+ domains.
13. Write a 14-day study plan.

## Validation

- Cleaned dataset is traceable to raw data.
- Findings are supported by metrics.
- Visuals are readable.
- Governance notes are practical.

## Review Questions

1. Which Data+ domain was hardest?
2. What would you improve in your dashboard?
3. Which data quality issue had the largest impact?
4. What is your first study action after this course?
